# Our preferences

These are the template owner's defaults. Friends: delete this file, or replace it with your own, before you use the template.

## Usual party
- **2 adults, 1 baby and our dog.** Some trips have extra people; don't assume them.
- **Always ask whether the dog and the baby are coming this time.** Don't assume either way.

## With the dog
- **Nervous dog:** every place to eat must be dog-friendly, and dinners should be spacious and relaxed (quiet corners, heated outdoor seating, roomy pubs and hotels).
- **Daily rhythm:** leave about 9am, dog walk in the morning, then lunch and an afternoon somewhere else, usually a town. Lunch isn't mid-walk.
- **Eating:** in on some nights, out on others.

## Without the dog
- **Walking:** we walk less.
- **Food:** we love it. Treat the trip as food-focused (see "Food-focused trips" in AGENTS.md), with more evening meals out.

## With the baby
- **Carriers:** we use carriers, so prams aren't needed and steeper walks are fine.
- **Dinners:** early (5–6pm).

## Always
- **Towns and villages** over paid attractions.
- **A list grouped by area,** with distances and what's near what. **Not** a day-by-day itinerary.
- **Drive time:** up to about 30 minutes.
- **Photos:** credit every photo. Freely licensed only.
- **Publishing:** a public repo on our own GitHub account.
